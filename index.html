About the Forum

Two days bringing together industry, researchers, research fellows and students to discuss the challenges facing construction, and to set the priorities ahead.

Topics are net zero across the board — including, but not limited to, the Hub's four research themes: digital twins and robotics, low-carbon concrete, construction and additive manufacturing, and asset operation and maintenance.

Hub project updates across all four research themes, blended with invited keynotes from outside the Hub.

About the Hub

This Forum is run by the ARC Research Hub for Transformation of Reclaimed Waste into Engineered Materials, based at Monash University and funded by the Australian Research Council.

The Hub works with industry partners to decarbonise the construction sector — through digital transformation, better manufacturing efficiency, and progress towards net-zero.
