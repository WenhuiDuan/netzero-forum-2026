Professor Pat Rajeev, Swinburne University of Technology

Leads the Hub's Operation and Maintenance theme — how infrastructure behaves after it is built, which is where most of the carbon and most of the cost actually sit. His recent review in Automation in Construction maps the shift from 3D to 4D printing in construction: components that change shape or property after they are placed.

He is bringing his industry partners with him.
