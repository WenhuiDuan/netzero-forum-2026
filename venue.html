Getting to Clayton campus

By public transport

Clayton campus is served by several bus routes, including the 601 shuttle from Huntingdale railway station. Huntingdale is on the Cranbourne and Pakenham lines from Melbourne's city centre, and the shuttle runs frequently on weekdays.

Allow about one hour from the city centre.

By car

Clayton campus is roughly 25 km south-east of central Melbourne, off Wellington Road. Visitor parking is available in the campus car parks and is paid. Please allow time to find a space.

Registered delegates have received parking information by email.

From Melbourne Airport

Approximately 45 to 60 minutes by taxi or rideshare, depending on traffic. There is no direct public transport route; travelling via the city centre takes about 1 hour 45 minutes.

Accommodation

Delegates arrange their own accommodation, unless the Hub has confirmed a booking for them.

Interstate and international delegates are encouraged to arrive on Sunday 8 November, when there is a welcome dinner.

Accessibility

The Forum rooms are on Level 4 of New Horizons. If you have specific access requirements, please email arcnetzero@monash.edu and we will make arrangements in advance.

Please tell us early rather than on the day — some arrangements take time to put in place.

Acknowledgement of Country
