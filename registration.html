Free to attend

Registration

The Forum is free, but places are limited and registration is required. Numbers are needed for catering, room capacity and the Hub dinner.

Register now

Registration closes Monday 26 October 2026. The form takes about a minute. You will get a confirmation email straight after submitting.

Presenting a talk? Register first, then follow the slide instructions further down this page.

Before you start

One page, about a minute. It will ask you for:

  • Your name and organisation
  • Your title or position, as you would like it on your name badge
  • Whether you are joining the Hub dinner
  • Whether you are presenting a talk
  • Any dietary requirements

You will get a confirmation email straight after submitting. If you are presenting, that email also carries the slide deadline and the instructions for sending your file.

Have an accessibility requirement? It is not on the form — please email arcnetzero@monash.edu directly and we will make arrangements in advance.

For speakers

If you are presenting

Slides are collected ahead of the Forum and loaded onto the room laptop in advance. This keeps the sessions to time and avoids swapping machines between talks.

Sending your slides

Email your slides to arcnetzero@monash.edu

Deadline: Monday 2 November 2026

You will receive a short reply confirming the file has arrived. If you do not hear back within two working days, please send a follow-up — do not assume it landed.

Subject line

Please use this exact format, so your file is not missed among other Hub correspondence:

[NZH Forum] Slides — Surname

File name

Name the file so it is identifiable on its own, once separated from your email:

NZH2026_Surname_ShortTitle.pptx

How your material is used

Slides sent to the Hub are retained by the Hub and may be used in reporting to the Australian Research Council, including the Hub's annual report. The registration form asks you to confirm you are content with this.

If your presentation contains unpublished results, commercially sensitive material, or content belonging to an industry partner, please tell us when you send the file. We can hold it as presentation-only and exclude it from any reporting use.

Slides are not published on this website unless we ask you separately and you agree.

Privacy

Registration details are collected by the ARC NetZero Hub at Monash University, and used only to run this event — catering, name badges, room capacity and event communications.

Dietary and accessibility information is shared with the venue and caterer only, and is deleted after the Forum. We do not pass your details to third parties. Contact the Hub if you would like your record removed.

Photography. A photographer works at the Forum on both days. Images are used only for the Hub's social media and for reporting to the Australian Research Council. They are not sold, licensed or passed to third parties. If you would prefer not to appear, email arcnetzero@monash.edu beforehand or tell the registration desk on the day, and we will let the photographer know.

Questions

Email arcnetzero@monash.edu. Registration and logistics are managed by the Hub Manager, Dr Felipe Basquiroto de Souza.
